Method

A score you can take apart.

No machine learning decides the verdict. Four extracted signals are fuzzified, evaluated against nineteen rules, aggregated and defuzzified into one number.

  1. 01

    Extract

    Keywords, links, capitals and punctuation are counted from the body.

  2. 02

    Fuzzify

    Each value gets a degree of membership in triangular and trapezoidal sets.

  3. 03

    Infer

    Nineteen Mamdani rules combine memberships with min and max.

  4. 04

    Defuzzify

    The aggregated output set is reduced to its centroid, 0–100.

All six output sets, drawn from the membership functions used in the score.
